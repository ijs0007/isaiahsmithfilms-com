# The offer, defined
Locked 11 September 2026. This is content, not design. It survives any rebuild of the page.

## What a shoot is, and what it is not

This has to be explicit on the page, in both directions. Vague scope makes Isaiah look
expensive for what he does, and it sets an expectation he cannot meet at this price.

NOT a commercial. A commercial has a script, a cast, a crew, lighting, and a stop-and-go
day where every shot is staged and reset. That is a different product with a different
price, and Isaiah Smith Films can quote it separately.

THIS is documentary style. One person shows up during a slow stretch. He films what is
already happening, asks the owner a few easy questions on camera, gets the B-roll, and
leaves. No actors. No script to memorise. No crew standing around. The business keeps
running the whole time.

Page wording to use:
  "No script, no actors, no crew standing around. I come during your slow hours, film
   what you already do, ask you a few questions, and get out of your way."

## The Intro — $1,200, one time

  One visit, about two hours, at your place
  One video, 60 to 90 seconds: who you are, what you do, why people come back
  Delivered horizontal (website, Google Business Profile, YouTube) AND vertical
    (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook)
  Three 15-second cutdowns to post over the following weeks
  Music, titles, your logo, your contact details
  Delivered within 7 days. One round of changes. The files are yours forever.

## Monthly Reels — $1,200 a month, cancel anytime

  One visit a month, about two hours
  FOUR videos, 20 to 40 seconds each, one to post each week
  Vertical first, horizontal versions where they are useful
  Five edited photographs for posts and your listing
  Captions written for you
  I decide what we shoot. We talk for ten minutes, then I come back with the plan.

Four setups from one visit. For a pizzeria that might be: the dough going in the oven,
her answering the question customers always ask, the Friday special, and one regular
saying why they keep coming back.

Why four short videos and no long one each month: the generic market package bundles a
60-120 second anchor into every month. A local business needs exactly one anchor and that
is The Intro. Paying for a new one every month is waste.

## Founding rate — $750

The Intro at $750 for the first three businesses, in exchange for showing the finished
video on the site and a short testimonial if they are happy. Against the $1,200 list
price this is a real discount and it reads as one.

## Simple on purpose

These are simple videos: real footage, music, titles. No motion graphics, no animation.
That is a positioning choice, not a limitation. The best local video looks real, not
slick, and the difference between real and amateur is the edit.

Anything bigger is a separate conversation and a separate quote under Isaiah Smith Films.
