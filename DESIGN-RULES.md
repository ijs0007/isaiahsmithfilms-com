# Design rules for isaiahsmithfilms.com/local
Adapted 11 Sept 2026 from the ruleset Isaiah wrote for Dream Lake. Same spine, this subject.

## 1. What this is
The front door for Isaiah Smith Films' local video offer. One person, sixteen years cutting
film and television, now making short videos for businesses nobody has noticed yet.
The raw material is the hero film, "Ask Your Editor", which Isaiah wrote, generated, comped
and cut himself. That film is the thing no competitor can copy and no template can supply.
It is the equivalent of Dream Lake's property photographs: everything visual on this page
should be traceable to it.

## 2. The organizing principle
Discovery first, commerce underneath. A visitor should meet the film, understand the idea
that being unnoticed is a fixable problem, and only then find out there are two ways to buy.
Do not lead with packages.

## 3. Where this brief fights itself
The film is dark, warm and cinematic. Isaiah asked for a page that is bright, white, open
and happy. That is not a contradiction to resolve, it is the design: the dark hero against
bright paper is the most interesting moment on the page. But it means the page's light
palette cannot be a generic cream. Derive every value from the film's own frames.

## 4. Role
Design lead at a small studio hired because the templated version of this page is worthless.
Reference frame: title design, film posters, editorial photography books, prescription and
clinical print ephemera, mid-century medical signage. NOT SaaS landing pages, agency sites,
freelancer portfolios or template marketplaces.

## 5. Ground everything in the actual subject
The subject is: a doctor who is really an editor, a pizzeria going quiet, a prescription pad,
a chart going down, golden hour, an oven. And underneath, a real business run by one person
out of Conyers who has cut for Disney and Netflix.
Named things are the most valuable material. "Ask Your Editor", "Heavenly Slice", "The Intro",
"Rx", "Refills: unlimited". Build the page's language out of those, not out of adjectives.
If a decision could be lifted onto another freelancer's site unnoticed, it is wrong.

## 6. Copy
Plain language, active voice, sentence case. A button says exactly what happens. No filler,
no selling adjectives. Ban the freelancer-video vocabulary the way Dream Lake bans the wedding
vocabulary: no "elevate your brand", no "storytelling that converts", no "in today's digital
landscape", no "let's create something amazing". Say what you do and what it costs.
No em dashes anywhere.

## 7. The offer must be unambiguous
See OFFER.md. Documentary style, not a commercial. Clip counts and lengths stated plainly.
Vague scope is a scope fight on the shoot day.

## 8. How to work
Stages, stop between them. Isaiah sets the step, Claude builds it, they prove it, then next.
  Stage 2  design plan BEFORE code: 4-6 named hex values each sourced from a named frame of
           the film; type candidates with roles and an argument each; layout in one sentence
           plus wireframe; principles.
  Stage 3  review the plan against itself. Would a generic prompt for "freelance videographer
           landing page" have produced this? Anything that survives that test unchanged is a
           default, not a choice. Revise, then say what changed.
  Stage 4  build.
  Stage 5  critique. Remove one thing before presenting.

## 9. Hard bans (the tells)
- Cream near #F4F1EA. Terracotta near #D97757.
- The SaaS card kit: identical rounded cards, one radius on everything, the same soft grey
  shadow under each element, gradient washes as decoration.
- Tracked out all caps eyebrow labels above headings.
- Meta strings joined with middle dots.
- WORD followed by a spaced em dash and a fragment.
- Tinted near black (#0B0B0B, #111) standing in for black.
- Monospace faces for small data labels.
- Arrows appended to link and button text.
- Accenting one word in a headline with italic, bold or a different colour.
- Numbered markers on content that is not a sequence.
- Fade and slide up entrances on every section; hover transitions on every card. One
  orchestrated motion moment beats scattered effects. Motion answering a user action is fine,
  ambient motion mostly is not.
- Purple to blue gradients, glassmorphism, mesh gradients, generic icon sets as content.
- Stock footage or stock photography of any kind. Everything is from the film or from a real
  client shoot.

ESCAPE CLAUSE. Any of these may be used if one sentence explains why it is right for that
specific content and Isaiah agrees. The ban is on reaching for it by reflex. If the argument
cannot be made, it was a reflex.

## 10. Quality floor, unannounced
Responsive. Visible keyboard focus. Reduced motion respected. Accessible contrast. Line length
under 80 characters. Real performance under video weight: the loop is 2.6MB and autoplays, the
full film is preload="none" and costs nothing until asked for. Never present this as an
achievement.

## 11. Working with Isaiah
Honest hard feedback, evidence not instinct, say plainly when he changes your mind. One step
at a time. Stop with a clear error rather than guess. Short and pointed, lead with a question.
