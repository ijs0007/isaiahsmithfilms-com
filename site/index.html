The Editor

About Isaiah

Isaiah Smith is a film and television editor based in Atlanta, Georgia. A proud member of the Motion Picture Editors Guild, Local 700, he has spent over a decade building his career cutting stories for some of the biggest names in the industry.

His editorial career was built working on shows for studios like Netflix, FX, Disney, and Freeform — working alongside talented editors and learning the craft from the ground up on some of the industry's most well-known productions.

Today, Isaiah brings that experience to every project he edits — mixing technical skill with a genuine feel for story, rhythm, and emotion.
