About Isaiah

Isaiah Smith is a film and television editor based in Atlanta, Georgia, and a member of the Motion Picture Editors Guild, Local 700. Sixteen years in post, as editor and assistant editor, on studio series, independent features, shorts and a pilot.

He has worked on series and features for Netflix, FX, Disney and Freeform, on real schedules and real deadlines.

He is at his best on material that rewards patience: performance, structure, and knowing when a scene is already working.
