Your Google listing

The panel people see before they ever reach your website

It is what appears when somebody searches your name, or searches for what you sell with the words near me on the end. Getting it created, getting it verified, and filling it out the way Google actually wants it filled out. Category, service areas, hours, services, photos, and the website link pointed at the page that answers what that person came for.

Straight with you. There are several ways to get a listing suspended and a suspended listing is slow and painful to get back. Putting a mailbox address in the location field is one. Stuffing keywords into the business name is another, and competitors report it. I know which ones to stay away from because I read the rules properly before I set up mine.

From youIt has to be created under your own Google login and Google verifies it against you personally, so we do that part together at your computer.
The waitVerification can take a few days before anything shows publicly. That is Google's clock, not mine.
ReviewsThe best time to ask a customer for one is the day you finish their job, and almost nobody does. I will set you up to ask.
A phone that answers

Miss the call, and they still hear from you

Most people who call a small business and get voicemail do not leave one. They hang up and call the next name on the list, and you never find out it happened.

So when a call comes in and nobody picks up, the caller gets a text from your business within seconds. They reply to it, that reply lands on your phone, and you answer when you get a minute. It is not a chatbot and nobody is pretending to be you. It is one automatic first text that holds the door open, and after that it is just you texting a customer.

Straight with you. Every US business sending automated texts has to be registered with the phone carriers first, and I do that paperwork. Mine took twelve days and two rejections before it cleared, so plan on a wait rather than a same day start. That wait is the reason this is worth paying someone for.

Three ways to set up the number

Which one fits depends on where your business is today. We work it out together on the first call.

A new business numberFor anyone still running the business off a personal cell. A real local number that belongs to the business, while your cell stays yours.
Keep the number you haveYour existing line forwards over only when nobody answers, so nothing changes for the people calling you. The text-back reaches them from the service number rather than the one they dialled.
Move your number acrossCalls and texts both live on your number and the customer sees one number throughout. Cleanest for them, longest to arrange.
